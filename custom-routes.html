Tailored to you

Every variable,
built around you.

Your objective

Recreational reef trip, deep wreck project, cave exploration, media or scientific work — the goal sets the plan.

Certification level

From newly qualified to full trimix and CCR. We build within your training, or fold instruction into the trip.

Timeline & pace

A long weekend or a multi-week expedition; relaxed diving or a hard push toward a single ambitious dive.

Destinations

One region or several stitched together — reefs, wrecks, caves, mines and rift lakes across the continent.

Support & safety

Guides, surface support, chase vessels, medic and oxygen cover — scaled to the risk and remoteness of the dive.

Gas & configuration

Air, nitrox, trimix and heliox; open circuit, sidemount or CCR — blended and configured for the objective.

Above the surface

Dive it, then
drive it.

Some of the best routes cross terrain worth taking slowly. We can build the whole expedition as an overland journey — self-driven or fully guided 4×4 — linking dive regions by road rather than by airport, with game parks, Rift Valley escarpments, crater lakes and quiet coast in between the water.

Coast to crater lakes

Drive inland from the Indian Ocean reefs to freshwater sites like Lake Chala on Kilimanjaro's flank — warm salt water and cold altitude freshwater in a single trip.

Dive & safari

Break the driving with Tsavo, Amboseli or the Rift Valley lakes — big-game country sits right on the roads between the dive regions.

Reach the remote

A 4×4 opens up water that fly-in trips can't — unsurveyed lakes, quiet stretches of coast, and border crossings run on our own schedule.

Vehicles & support

Equipped 4×4s, rooftop tents or lodges, compressor and fill support on the move, and a driver-guide who knows the roads and the paperwork.

In Cousteau's Wake

RWA / COD · Rift lakes

The Rift Lakes

Reefs & Walls

Warm-water reefs and historic wrecks in Kenya, south through Tanzania's Pemba and Mafia channel walls, then on to Mozambique's oceanic pinnacles and deep drop-offs.
