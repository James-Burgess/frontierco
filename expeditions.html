Expedition Support

We make the
impossible — logistical.

Deep Frontier provides the operational backbone for scientific, documentary and record-attempt expeditions in places where no established dive infrastructure exists. You bring the objective — we build everything around it, from the first permit to the last decompression stop.

What we support

Expeditions with
a purpose.

Every expedition is planned, documented and executed with professional rigour — whatever gets your team into the water and back out safely.

Research

Scientific research

We put marine biologists, geologists and archaeologists on the sites they need — sample collection, survey and monitoring at depths and in environments well beyond recreational reach.

Media

Film & documentation

Camera and lighting support and rebreather operations for documentary and broadcast productions. We have coordinated underwater cinematography across multiple African projects.

Survey

Shipwreck & artefact survey

Survey methodology, photogrammetry dives and sample recovery — all conducted within national maritime law and with full permitting documentation in place.

Exploration

Record & exploration

First-descent and record-attempt support in locations with no dive infrastructure at all — where the entire operation has to be conceived, provisioned and built from nothing.

The operational backbone

Everything the dive
rests on.

The dive is the visible part. Underneath it sits a whole operation — the logistics, safety and self-sufficiency that let a team work far from any established support.

01

Permitting & logistics

National permits, port clearances, customs and the paperwork that gets a team and its gear to the water legally.

02

Surface support & vessels

Chase boats, dive platforms and support craft chartered and crewed for the demands of the site.

03

Gas & fill stations

On-site blending of nitrox, trimix and heliox, and portable fill-station setup for remote operations.

04

Decompression & safety

In-water decompression stations, oxygen and stage-gas management, and full DAN-affiliated emergency planning.

05

Medical & O₂ cover

Medic and oxygen first-aid cover on site, with evacuation routes mapped and rehearsed before the first dive.

06

Remote self-sufficiency

Power, compressors, communications and spares — everything needed to run an operation where nothing exists.
