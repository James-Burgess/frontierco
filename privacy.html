Privacy Policy — Deep Frontier Dive

Effective 3 July 2026 · Applies to the Deep Frontier Dive app for iPhone and iPad

Deep Frontier Dive collects no data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. The app contains no analytics or advertising frameworks and requests no device permissions.

Your dive plans and settings

Dive plans, gas presets, and settings are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded anywhere. If your device is set to back up app data via iCloud or your computer, those backups are handled by Apple under your own device settings; we have no access to them. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Purchases

The optional "Dive Tools Pro" unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name, or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Network use

The app works fully offline. Its only network activity is the App Store purchase and receipt check performed by Apple's StoreKit system framework.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children — it collects no data at all.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated here before that version is released, and the app's App Store privacy label will be updated to match.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact form at deepfrontier.co.
