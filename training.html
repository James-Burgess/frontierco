Extended Range Foundations
The groundwork every technical dive is built on

Before depth, before decompression, before gas — control. Foundations is a workshop in the fundamentals that separate technical divers from everyone else: precise trim and buoyancy, efficient propulsion, calm valve and gas-sharing drills, and a properly configured twinset or sidemount rig. No decompression, no depth extension — just the discipline that makes everything above it safe.

You'll master

  • Horizontal trim and fine buoyancy control
  • Frog, back and helicopter propulsion kicks
  • Valve drills, S-drills and gas sharing as a team
  • Backmount twinset or sidemount configuration

Extended Range Nitrox
Your first staged decompression certification

This is where you cross the line into true technical diving — planned, staged decompression. You'll learn to run a dive with a real deco obligation to 40 metres, carrying and switching to nitrox and up to 100% oxygen as decompression gases. Ascent discipline, gas planning, and contingency thinking move from theory to muscle memory.

You'll master

  • Decompression theory and dive planning
  • Accelerated deco with rich nitrox and pure O₂
  • Stage cylinder handling and gas switches
  • Managed ascents and problem-solving at stops

Extended Range
Trimix, and the real threshold of technical diving

At 45 metres, air is no longer an honest gas — narcosis and gas density become the limiting factors. Extended Range introduces helium into the mix so your head stays clear while the workload climbs. You'll plan and execute multi-gas decompression dives with the redundancy, discipline and calm that deeper diving demands.

You'll master

  • Trimix blending logic and gas planning
  • Managing narcosis and gas density with helium
  • Multi-gas decompression across several cylinders
  • Team protocols and failure management at depth

Technical Extended Range
Normoxic trimix and self-sufficient team diving

The step beyond Extended Range. Bottom mixes get richer in helium and leaner in oxygen, decompression obligations grow, and the team becomes genuinely self-sufficient — each diver carrying and managing their own bailout and stage gas. You'll plan and run longer mixed-gas dives to 60 metres where nothing is improvised and every contingency has an answer worked out on the surface first.

You'll master

  • Normoxic trimix planning and gas logistics
  • Extended, multi-gas decompression schedules
  • Independent, self-sufficient team protocols
  • Failure management and bailout at depth

Hypoxic Trimix
The deepest rung of the open-circuit ladder

The deep end of open-circuit diving. Past 60 metres a breathable bottom mix has too little oxygen to be breathed safely at the surface, so you'll add a travel gas and manage a stack of cylinders through descent, bottom time and a long staged ascent. This is dive planning, gas management and team discipline at their most demanding — hypoxic bottom mixes and multiple decompression gases, all the way to 100 metres.

You'll master

  • Hypoxic bottom-mix blending and planning
  • Travel-gas and multi-stage cylinder handling
  • Long, staged decompression to the surface
  • Deep team protocols and contingency planning
