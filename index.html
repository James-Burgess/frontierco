Who we are

Built by divers,
for the frontier.

Deep Frontier Diving was founded by a team of technical divers with extensive experience across East African waters. Our focus is on delivering safe, meticulously planned dive operations — whether a guided technical trip or full logistical support for a large-scale expedition.

We operate with rebreathers, trimix, heliox, and advanced stage diving configurations. Every operation is planned, documented, and executed with professional rigour.

Safety First
Thorough dive planning, proper preparation, and contingency protocols on every operation.
Local Expertise
Deep relationships with local operators, port authorities, and communities across East Africa.
Technical Capability
CCR, OC, trimix, sidemount — we configure for the objective, not the other way around.

Our mark is the cuneiform glyph for the open water — humanity's oldest writing, pressed into clay to record the first ventures beyond the known. We took it as our own: a record of going where the map runs out.

Where we dive

Africa's defining
dive frontiers.

Every destination is selected for its technical diving merit — depth, visibility, structure, and the challenge it presents.

MOZ · 11°S
Mozambique
Quirimbas Archipelago
Pristine oceanic walls, deep pinnacles, and Raggie sharks along structures that drop beyond 80m.
ZAF · 30°S
South Africa
Aliwal Shoal · Sodwana Bay
World-class wreck diving, Tiger shark dives, and the Cathedral — a complex technical cave system.
ZWE · 17°S
Zimbabwe
Chinhoyi Caves
Vertical limestone shafts, deep cave passages, and unsurveyed leads in some of Africa's least-dived karst.
RWA / COD · 2°S
Rwanda / DRC
Lake Kivu
High-altitude freshwater diving in a volcanically active rift lake with exceptional visibility.
NAM · 19°S
Namibia
Dragon's Breath · Aigamas
Cathedral-scale sinkholes, abseil-access underground lakes, and unsurveyed passages dropping past 140m.
SDN · 19°N
Sudan
Conshelf II · Sanganeb
Diving in Cousteau's footsteps — Conshelf II on the reef, deep wartime wrecks, and southern walls dropping below diveable depths.
KEN · 2°S
Kenya
Lamu · Diani
Historic wrecks, coral-encrusted drop-offs, and warm Indian Ocean water year-round.
